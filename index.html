          About QR Studio
          
Create QR codes for links, Wi-Fi, contact details, and more, or scan QR codes and common barcodes directly on your device.

        

        

          Privacy policy
          
Your settings and history stay in this browser's local storage. History includes full QR contents, which may contain details such as Wi-Fi passwords; anyone with access to this browser may be able to read them. Remove individual entries or clear history when needed. Scans are decoded on-device and are not uploaded. When you generate a QR code, its content is sent to QuickChart to create the image; avoid encoding sensitive information. Clearing this browser's site data removes saved settings and history.

        

        

          Help
          
Choose a content type, enter the details, and select Generate QR code. To scan, allow camera access after pressing Start camera, or choose an image. Camera scanning needs HTTPS or localhost. Use the history buttons to copy a saved code or remove it.
